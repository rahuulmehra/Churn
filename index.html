The problem we solve

In one line: Churnio is a website that tells an online shopping company (like Amazon or Flipkart) which customers are about to stop buying, why, and what offer to give them so they stay.

On shopping apps, customers leave very easily: a late delivery, an unsolved complaint, or a better offer elsewhere is enough. Companies usually notice only after the customer is gone, and they send the same coupon to everyone. That wastes money on happy customers and does too little for the ones about to leave.

Our solution in 3 steps

1. Predict

A machine learning model (SVM) evaluates 18 customer behavioral signals and outputs an exact chance of leaving (e.g. 99.5%).

2. Explain

The system automatically pinpoints why: e.g. "new customer", "unresolved complaint", or "lower cashback compared to average".

3. Act

The probability translates into 1 of 5 targeted risk tiers with automated ready offers, from regular loyalty points up to a 20% comeback discount.

What we found in the data (Key Statistical Drivers)

Who leaves the most How much What a company should do
New customers (first 3 months) 41% leave (vs 5% after 1 year) Welcome offers, smooth first delivery
Customers who complained 31% leave (vs 11%) Solve complaints fast, follow up immediately
Mobile Phone buyers 26% leave Check prices and delivery warranty in that category
Cash on Delivery users 25% leave Extra cashback incentive for switching to online payment

Business insights

Customers who raised a complaint are 2.6x more likely to churn (32% vs 12%).
Action: Solve complaints faster and follow up with a call after every complaint.
New customers (0-3 months) have the highest risk: 42% churn vs 18% overall.
Action: Give a welcome offer on the 2nd and 3rd order and make the first delivery experience smooth.
Product category 'Mobile Phone' has the highest churn rate: 25% vs 18% overall.
Action: Check prices, delivery time and offers in the 'Mobile Phone' category.
City tier '3' has the highest churn rate: 24% vs 18% overall.
Action: Improve delivery speed and local offers in City Tier 3.
Marital status 'Single' has the highest churn rate: 36% vs 18% overall.
Action: Create targeted offers for 'Single' customers.
Customers likely to churn get less cashback on average (Rs 167 vs Rs 178).
Action: Increase cashback for at-risk customers on their next order.
18% of customers are Critical risk (35) and 0% are High risk (0).
Action: Start the priority retention campaign with Critical customers (20% discount + priority support), then High risk customers (15% discount + personalized offer).
